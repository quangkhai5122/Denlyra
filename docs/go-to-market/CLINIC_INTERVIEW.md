# Design-partner clinic observation and interview protocol

**No real patient identifiers in this repository, coding assistant or transcription service.** Obtain permission and consent for observation, use fictional cases or on-site redacted notes and approved storage.

## Participants per partner clinic
Owner, licensed dentist, assistant, receptionist, treatment coordinator, accounting/cashier, inventory manager if present, lab partner if possible; at least one clinic currently on paper and one using competing software.

## Clinic operating context (coded privately)
License type (specialist clinic/hospital), outpatient scope, Sở Y tế jurisdiction, number of chairs, dentists, shifts, software, imaging equipment, paper forms, clinical signers, legal retention responsibilities, lab workflow and billing/provider integrations. Record exact source of clinic-specific requirement and human reviewer; never infer from national summaries.

## Interview (recent-work evidence)
1. Walk through last new outpatient visit: where was the form created, who read/signed, how long, what was re-entered?
2. Show last multi-stage treatment; how does team know next required step, person, lab readiness and payment status?
3. Show last lab delay or remake; who learned first, how was appointment corrected, who bore cost?
4. Show last appointment conflict/cancellation; what information was absent and what resulted?
5. Show last end-of-day cash reconciliation; which system is source of truth, what was copied to Excel?
6. What existing software is used? What are exact shortcomings in recorded workflows, not generic dislike?
7. What happens when internet or clinic PC fails? What was latest tested restore?
8. How are old paper files located and how is missing information handled?
9. How are EMR laws/clinic license and signing rules interpreted by health authority/counsel?
10. Who decides software purchase, onboarding help, data migration and billing; what is paid today?
11. Will clinic execute a demo test with synthetic scenario? Under what condition would it pay for a pilot?
12. Which step is strictly professional, and which may be automated without clinical interpretation?

## Deliverable
Per participant: role, task map, number of handoffs, observed errors, time/cost baseline, software versions, legal unknowns and candidate success metric; no PHI. Aggregate across clinics, mark facts vs hypotheses, identify blockers requiring counsel/dentist approval.
