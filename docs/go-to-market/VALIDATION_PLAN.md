# Validation plan and competitor benchmark

## Competitive baseline (2026-11-17)
Domestic dental SaaS: DentalSoft, TDental, DentalFlow, SimlyDent, VTTech, DentIQ. International dental practice/ortho enterprise: Planet DDS/Dentrix/Open Dental and niche lab/clinical monitoring. Source verified capabilities and prices must be redone during trials; do not assume missing website screenshots equal absent functionality.

## Three competing hypotheses
- H1: A clinically accurate, paper-to-EMR migration + signature/backup process is a current buying trigger at licensed outpatient dental clinics.
- H2: Multi-stage treatment cases create handoff/coordination work across physician, assistant, front desk, lab and finance not well handled in existing systems.
- H3: Staff will switch to a system that demonstrably decreases daily duplicate entry and clinical admin errors without putting records at risk.

## Evidence plan
1. 15 clinic interviews across 5 paper-first, 5 incumbent-DMS, 5 specialty multi-stage (overlap possible, explicitly report).
2. Benchmark with dental software alternatives using same synthetic scenarios (UJ-01..06) and audited feature capabilities.
3. Record workflow bottlenecks and 95% confidence intervals on repeated timings when data size permits; early samples only descriptive.
4. Ask for conditional paid pilot based on measurable outcomes rather than hypothetical product approval.
5. Pilot clinic must test data export and mock disaster recovery; a product with unexportable charts fails regardless of UI.

## Commercial differentiation to falsify
An all-in-one feature catalog is not a moat. Verify (a) transfer speed and safety, (b) quality of treatment/case coordination, (c) depth of audit and data lineage, (d) staff onboarding and mobile workflow, (e) approved secure patient communication. If two existing competitors perform better, narrow to a verified unsolved workflow.

## Sales and legal caution
Do not imply that Denlyra already meets 13/2025/TT-BYT, that every clinic must stop paper on 1 Jan 2027 without legal classification, or that a deadline proves customers should buy a newly coded system. Compliance timelines must be sourced in official law and confirmed by local authority/counsel.
