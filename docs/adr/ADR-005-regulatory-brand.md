# ADR-005 — Site-specific EMR/legal gate and product-name caution
Date 2026-11-17 · Status Accepted

**Decision:** No real patient data or “EMR compliant” launch before clinic license/outpatient scope verified, official template mapping and signatures reviewed by dentist/counsel, security and DR validated. Circular 13/2025 contains a 31 Dec 2026 deadline for certain non-hospital outpatient facilities; never simplify to blanket “1 Jan 2027 everyone stops paper” without clinic classification and Sở Y tế guidance.
**Brand (historical caution; naming superseded by ADR-006):** The original project codename was not commercially cleared. Current codename Denlyra also requires independent commercial brand review.
**Consequence:** Legal discovery and clinical owner are first-class deliverables; no deadline-based pressure tactics.
