# Competitive strategy & brand caution

**Research checkpoint: 2026-11-17. Competitor capabilities may change.** DentalSoft, TDental, DentalFlow, SimlyDent, VTTech and international Dentrix/Planet DDS already provide much of the functional foundation; verify exact SKU/features via actual demos rather than marketing pages.

## Critical branding warning
Denlyra is the current project codename, not a commercially cleared brand. Complete brand/IP review before a product launch. Third-party product names and source URLs refer to competitors, never to this project. The naming change is recorded in ADR-006.

## Competitive dimensions to test
- Time to onboard a clinic, clinic-specific form templates and paper record migration quality.
- Speed of front-desk calendar, doctor/assistant workflows and odontogram usability.
- Integrity/traceability across treatment plan, lab, payment and patient communication.
- Availability of reviewed clinical record exports, signature provider and clinic-level compliance assurance.
- Actual integration supported with imaging, messaging, payments and fiscal invoicing.
- Local support quality, outage/restore behavior, role access and cost of switching.
- Whether users duplicate entries in spreadsheets/other apps after adopting incumbent.

## Differentiation hypothesis
One evidence-aware timeline per treatment: authored plan versions → dependent staff/lab steps → clinic resource booking → patient acknowledgment → payments and follow-up. Owner can see missed handoffs, doctor sees clinical source, patient sees only approved content.

## Go-to-market tests
Shadow 5 actual visits at each of 2–3 clinics, compare real incumbent workflows and measure staff time/errors; do not collect identifying PHI in notebooks. Require conditional pilot agreements and real willingness-to-pay signals before funding full all-in-one rewrite. A documented feature missing from marketing is not proof incumbent lacks it.

## Risks
Cheap full-feature competitors, migration resistance, clinical compliance liability, time to onboarding, staff training, service-level expectations and potential brand conflicts. Avoid generic AI-chatbot differentiation.
